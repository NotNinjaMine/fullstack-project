## Format

Each entry records, in order:

1. **What I asked** — the actual instruction given
2. **What the AI did** — including tool use and what it inspected
3. **What came back** — the substantive output
4. **My decision** — what I accepted, rejected, or changed, and why

Points where the AI was **wrong** are recorded as prominently as the points where
it was useful. Those are the entries that show judgement was applied rather than
output being pasted in unread.
