Sample medical certificate generator

Testing aid for UC-13's AI certificate check. Fill in the dates, click Download, then attach the PNG to a sick-leave request in the app and press “Check MC with AI”. Not a real certificate — no patient data.


To see a MATCH: use the same dates as your leave request.
To see a MISMATCH: use dates a week away from your request — the app should tell you the certificate does not cover the days you applied for.
